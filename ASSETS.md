## Rendering

`ProductImage` uses Next Image with explicit dimensions, responsive `sizes` and reserved layouts. All rendered product files use Next's AVIF/WebP delivery. The hero is preloaded; reused hero frames share that request, and other below-fold images use lazy loading. No external image domains are configured.

Archived neutral drawings are reproducible with `node scripts/create-studies.mjs`. Their fictional labels belong only to those unused maquettes.
